# 🚨 RoadSoS v2.0 — AI Emergency Road Safety Assistant
### Road Safety Hackathon 2026 · CoERS, IIT Madras · BIMSTEC Countries

---

## 🎯 Overview
RoadSoS is an AI-powered emergency chatbot providing **instant, location-based life-saving assistance** during road accidents across all 7 BIMSTEC countries.

---

## ✨ AI Features (Innovation Highlights)

### 🔴 AI Severity Detection
Natural language accident descriptions are analyzed by Claude AI to classify severity (CRITICAL/HIGH/MODERATE/LOW), detect injuries, identify required services, and generate immediate action steps.

### ⏱️ Golden Hour AI
The most critical road safety window. RoadSoS calculates nearest trauma center ETA, assesses whether the victim is within the golden hour, and provides urgency-calibrated guidance.

### 🌐 Multilingual Emergency Translation
Translates emergency messages into all 7 BIMSTEC regional languages: Hindi, Bengali, Sinhala, Nepali, Burmese, Thai, Dzongkha.

### 🩹 AI-Generated First Aid
Dynamic, situation-specific first aid steps generated by AI — not static templates.

### 🎤 Voice/Crash Detection Mode
Detects emergency keywords from voice transcripts and auto-triggers the emergency response protocol.

### 📊 Analytics Dashboard
Tracks and visualizes incident data by country, severity, and service type.
